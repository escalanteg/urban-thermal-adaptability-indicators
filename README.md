# Urban Thermal Adaptability Index (UTAI)
Master's thesis: "Assessing Urban Thermal Adaptation with Open Global Data: Bologna–Córdoba Comparative Study"

##	General Objectives
To develop a novel Urban Thermal Adaptation Index (UTAI) based on the estimation of Mean Radiant Temperature (MRT) using high-resolution UAV-derived data, and to assess the feasibility of estimating this index through open global datasets by conducting a comparative analysis in Bologna (Italy) and exploring its transferability to cities with limited high-resolution data availability.

## Specific Objectives
- To identify and map the variables that influence the thermal comfort of people in different spaces of Bologna’s city as a reference case.
- To build a novel index based on Thermal Adaptability of urban spaces (Urban Thermal Adaptability Index) with the identified variables. 
- To compute the UTAI index using global datasets and evaluate its deviation from the HR-based UTAI in Bologna.
- To transfer the index to Córdoba, Argentina, using the available global datasets, and analyze its potential performance.
